The autumn cohort

Complete, approve and ratify your Use of AI Policy this term, working through it with me and a small group of schools, trusts and colleges.

Home › The autumn cohort

A template on its own isn’t a policy. The cohort is where yours gets completed, agreed and ratified: five sessions across the autumn term, done by Christmas.

What it is

Twelve organisations at most, meeting online across the autumn term: five taught sessions of ninety minutes. Bring your digital lead, your DPO or DSL, and a senior leader: whoever carries the policy in your context.

Each session works a slice of the policy, in the order the template does: scope and accountability, tools and acceptable use, data and impact assessment (including a live DPIA screening on a real product), safeguarding and oversight, then ratification. You bring your policy as it stands; you leave each session knowing exactly what to complete before the next one, with the workbook to guide it.

Sessions aren’t recorded, deliberately, so people can speak freely about where their organisation actually is. The workbook and weekly round-up carry everything you need if you have to miss one. The programme finishes in November so your policy reaches your governors or trustees before Christmas.

What you get

The template gives you the words. The cohort gets them completed, agreed and ratified. One price buys:

  • A completed policy, through your stakeholders and ratified by your board, in force for the spring term. Everything below serves that.
  • Five taught sessions covering every section of the policy, in the order the template does.
  • Me, every week, all term: send your questions in each Friday and I answer them for the cohort.
  • A live DPIA screening on a real product, start to finish, in session three.
  • The cohort workbook and ratification pack: the section-by-section completion checklist, and the model paper for taking the policy to your governors or trustees.
  • For large trusts: a private session with me on your trust-specific rollout.
